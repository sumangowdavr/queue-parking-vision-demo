- **Ultralytics YOLO11 and ByteTrack integration:** https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics and https://docs.ultralytics.com/ . The recorded package was Ultralytics 8.3.40, AGPL-3.0. Fine-tuned weights derive from YOLO11n and YOLO11n-OBB pretrained weights. Original code in this repository is released under the accompanying AGPL-3.0 license; third-party data and media retain their own terms.
- **PKLot:** Almeida, P., Oliveira, L. S., Silva Jr, E., Britto Jr, A., Koerich, A. *PKLot – A robust dataset for parking lot classification*, Expert Systems with Applications, 42(11):4937–4949, 2015. Dataset and terms: https://web.inf.ufpr.br/vri/databases/parking-lot-database/ . CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Retrieved through https://huggingface.co/datasets/Voxel51/PKLot . Included annotations were converted to YOLO oriented boxes; demonstration frames were annotated, resized and encoded into videos. The three camera views are PUCPR, UFPR04 and UFPR05.
- **CrowdHuman:** Shao, S. et al. *CrowdHuman: A Benchmark for Detecting Human in a Crowd*, arXiv:1805.00123, 2018. https://www.crowdhuman.org/ and https://www.crowdhuman.org/download.html . Retrieved through https://huggingface.co/datasets/ShantyCam/crowdhuman . The original dataset terms limit use to non-commercial research and education and prohibit redistributing images. No CrowdHuman images or montage plots are included here. The trained people checkpoint is intended for non-commercial research and educational demonstrations; it is not a commercial-use clearance.
- **Queue demonstration footage:** Coverr, *Queue to Carrefour Market in Paris*, source filename `coverr-queue-to-carrefour-market-in-paris-3150.mp4`. https://coverr.co/videos/queue-to-carrefour-market-in-paris-3150 . Terms: https://coverr.co/license . Used as inference-only input, with detection boxes, tracking IDs, zone, dwell/count display and alert overlay added, then resized and re-encoded.
